Targeted Impact

Where Your Support Goes

Every contribution directly finances vital aspects of the Students Parliament program, giving young Nigerian students the platform they need to excel.

Televised Studio Broadcasts

Funds camera production, sound engineering, multi-angle coverage, and airtime broadcast so school parliamentary debates reach living rooms across Nigeria.

Interschool Parliamentary Contests

Sponsors student delegations, interschool legislative tourneys, debate maces, and transportation for student parliamentarians visiting official assemblies.

Civic Literacy & Leadership Kits

Supplies legislative rulebooks, public speaking guides, and constitution handbooks to establish vibrant Students Parliament Clubs in government and private secondary schools.

Day Parliamentarian Awards

Honors standout student speakers with medals, leadership certificates, and the prestigious opportunity to serve as an honorary Day Speaker or Day Parliamentarian.
